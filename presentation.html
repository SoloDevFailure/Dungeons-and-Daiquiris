THE RULES OF THE TAVERN

  1. Ⅰ

    Roll three D20s

    Each roll chooses an ingredient.

  2. Ⅱ

    Meet the abomination

    One questionable cocktail. Two servings.

  3. Ⅲ

    Know the stakes

    Each drink gets starting points and a bidding range.

  4. Ⅳ

    Auction the honour

    Negotiate from half to double the starting points.

  5. Ⅴ

    Claim your serving

    Up to two drinkers. The same agreed points each.

  6. Ⅵ

    Drink. Score. Win.

    Drinkers earn the points. Highest total wins the prizes.
